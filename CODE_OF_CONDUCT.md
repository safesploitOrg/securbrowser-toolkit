# Code of conduct

SecurBrowser Toolkit welcomes constructive technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, avoid harassment or discriminatory behaviour, and treat vulnerability reports responsibly.

Disagreement is normal. Keep discussion focused on the code, design, evidence and user impact rather than the person proposing a change.

Maintainers may moderate or remove abusive, disruptive, threatening or deliberately harmful participation. Serious conduct concerns can be raised privately with the project maintainers.
